Downside of Valgrind is because it has to look at the instructions as they're being executed, it's a lot slower. Valgrind will slow down your app by a factor of ten or some big number like that. The advantage of Valgrind though, is that you're going to catch mistakes that you wouldn't easily catch otherwise.
